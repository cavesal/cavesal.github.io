🔓 How do I activate the full version?

CaVeSal® · Maternal Fetal Intelligence

ESENPT
📱 Using an iPhone or iPad? Before activating your key, install the app first: tap the share icon in Safari → "Add to Home Screen" → open the app from that installed icon → only then enter your key. If you activate in Safari before installing, you will not be able to reuse the key when you open the installed app.

📊 CaVeSal® Prenatal Calculator

1

Buy your key on Gumroad, choosing the "Calculadora" version.

2

Within minutes you will receive an email with your access key (format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).

3

Open cavesal.app/full/ on the device where you will use it, paste your key when prompted and tap "Activar" (Activate).

4

Done — it is permanently unlocked on that device, with all future updates included.

📋 CaVeSal® Clinical Record

1

Buy your key on Gumroad, choosing the "Historia Clínica" version.

2

Within minutes you will receive an email with 2 keys: one for the PC/office and one for your phone. Both devices sync through Google Drive.

3

Open historia.cavesal.app on each device (one key per device), tap any padlock 🔒, paste your key and tap "Activar" (Activate).

4

Done — the 17 clinical guides, plotted curves, export and direct sending to the Calculator are unlocked.

📋 Using it for the first time? See the full Clinical Record tutorial →

⭐ Calculator + Clinical Record Combo

1

Buy the "Combo" on Gumroad — it is the best-priced option if you want both apps.

2

You will receive a single email with 3 keys: 1 for the Calculator and 2 for the Clinical Record (PC/office + phone).

3

Activate each key in its corresponding app, following the same steps above.

💡 Each key works on a single device and can be activated only once — make sure you activate each one in the right app.

🤖 CaVeSal® IA

Unlike the professional apps, CaVeSal® IA is not activated with a padlock inside the app — it is a personal account (email + PIN) that you then use from any device.

1

Buy your plan on Gumroad, choosing "Pro", "Premium" or "Premium Plus" depending on the number of queries you need.

2

Within minutes you will receive an email with your activation code.

3

Open ia.cavesal.app, enter the code and your email, and choose the PIN you will use from then on (any PIN you like, to identify yourself). You also choose the response language (Spanish, English or Portuguese) — it is saved with your account.

4

Done — from then on you sign in with your email and PIN from any device (no need to enter the code again), and the "Analizar con CaVeSal® IA" (Analyze with CaVeSal® IA) button becomes available in both the Calculator and the Clinical Record.

🤖 Using it for the first time? See the full CaVeSal® IA tutorial →, with plans, daily quotas and how top-ups work if you run out of your daily quota.
